# Changelog

**Every entry names the failure that caused the change.** What moved is in the diff
already; why it moved is the part that does not survive anywhere else, and it is what tells
a later reader whether the change still applies to them.

Nothing is listed until it is merged. Work in an open pull request belongs in the pull
request, because a changelog that describes intentions is one nobody can trust.
